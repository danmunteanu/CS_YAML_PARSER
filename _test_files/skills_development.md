# Transferable Skills for the 2025 Economy

As technical tools evolve at breakneck speeds, **transferable skills**—the abilities that move with you across industries—have become the ultimate career insurance.

### Critical Core Competencies
* **Critical Thinking:** As AI handles data processing, humans are required for high-stakes decision-making in ambiguous "grey areas."
* **Emotional Intelligence (EQ):** The ability to lead, resolve conflict, and build trust remains uniquely human.
* **Agentic Literacy:** Knowing how to effectively prompt, guide, and audit AI systems is the new professional baseline.
* **Adaptability:** A "learning to learn" mindset allows workers to pivot as specific frameworks become obsolete.

### Why It Matters
Focusing on these skills ensures long-term value. While a specific software library may fade, the ability to solve complex problems and communicate effectively is evergreen.
